# S003 Donchian breakout research

S003 is a trend-following experiment using Donchian channels and risk controls. Historical tests use separate in-sample and previously examined out-of-sample windows, with slippage, fees, and funding considered. Historical results do not establish future or live performance.

The original research notes are retained locally. Reproduction requires the matching market catalog, funding data, software environment, and recorded run manifests; these data and generated artifacts are not distributed in this repository. Funding is applied to the trade list after simulation and does not compound through the equity curve.
